## Technical SEO Enhancements for Premium

### 1. Create Premium Landing Page
**URL**: `/premium` or `/features`

**Structure:**
```
- Hero: "Your Learning, Hyper-Personalized"
- Problem: Why generic roadmaps don't work
- Solution: How our AI customizes for YOU
- Features: 3-column grid with icons
- Pricing: Clear tiers and comparison
- Social Proof: Testimonials + ratings
- FAQ: Address objections
- CTA: "Start Your Personalized Journey"
```

**SEO Elements:**
- Title: "Acad AI Premium - Hyper-Personalized Learning Roadmaps"
- Meta: Focus on transformation + results
- Schema: Product, Offer, Review aggregation
- Internal links from all roadmap pages

### 2. Pricing Page SEO
**URL**: `/pricing`

**Content:**
- Free tier benefits (keep users happy)
- Premium features with visual comparisons
- "Most popular" badge on recommended tier
- Annual discount option
- Money-back guarantee
- No hidden fees messaging

**Schema Markup:**
```json
{
  "@type": "Product",
  "name": "Acad AI Premium",
  "offers": {
    "@type": "AggregateOffer",
    "priceCurrency": "USD",
    "lowPrice": "0",
    "highPrice": "29.99"
  }
}
```

### 3. Testimonials Page
**URL**: `/success-stories`

- Individual case studies
- Learning time comparisons
- Career outcome improvements
- Star ratings and quotes
- Video testimonials

**Schema**: Review + Rating markup

## Competitive SEO Analysis

### Main Competitors:
1. **Roadmap.sh**
   - Strength: Community-driven, established
   - Weakness: Not personalized
   - Opportunity: Target "personalized" keywords they don't own

2. **Coursera/Udemy Paths**
   - Strength: Brand recognition
   - Weakness: Course-locked, expensive
   - Opportunity: "Free personalized roadmap" angle

3. **GitHub Learning Paths**
   - Strength: Developer trust
   - Weakness: Static, not adaptive
   - Opportunity: "AI-powered adaptation"

### Differentiation Strategy:
**Never compete on "roadmap" alone**
Always add: personalized, adaptive, AI-powered, custom, tailored
